# Competitive Positioning

Position by **category**, not by unsupported vendor attacks.

SignalForge hypothesis: engineering leaders need **delivery readiness
intelligence** that combines capability modeling, dependency graph risk,
counterfactual overlays, and grounded executive briefs — with tenant isolation
and human review.

## Claims avoided

- “The only platform”  
- “Best in market” / “industry-leading”  
- Named-competitor feature deficiencies without evidence

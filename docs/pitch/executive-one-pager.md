# SignalForge Executive One-Pager

**Category:** AI-native enterprise engineering execution intelligence  
**Status discipline:** IMPLEMENTED vs POC CONFIGURATION vs PROPOSED vs DEFERRED

## Mission

Help engineering leaders answer: *Can this team successfully deliver this
initiative — and what evidence-backed risks threaten readiness?*

## The problem

Delivery decisions are made from fragmented signals: spreadsheets, intuition,
status decks, and disconnected analytics. Leaders miss capability gaps,
dependency risk, ownership concentration, and key-person exposure until late.

## What SignalForge does (IMPLEMENTED)

- Deterministic readiness and confidence scoring with decision traces  
- Capability coverage, skill-gap, and key-person risk analysis  
- Team simulation and human review that never rewrites scores  
- Delivery Graph findings with evidence references  
- Delivery prediction with honest fallback (`uncalibrated_score` ≠ probability)  
- Counterfactual scenario overlays (decision-support, not causal claims)  
- AI Chief of Staff briefs with claims, citations, and grounding controls  
- Default-deny JWT auth, RBAC, audit, PostgreSQL FORCE RLS, observability  

## Who it is for

- Economic buyer: CTO / VP Engineering  
- Operational buyer: Engineering Operations / Program leadership  
- Users: directors, managers, platform and architecture leaders  

**Not for:** employee surveillance, automated employment decisions, or PR-count
productivity scoring.

## NovaBank demonstration

Fictional synthetic tenant for demos and tests only — not a real bank or
customer. Seed + materialize produces initiatives, graph findings, 8 scenarios,
and Chief-of-Staff briefs for product tours.

## Microsoft enterprise POC

A 4–6 week evaluation blueprint exists for customer tenants (connectors,
security review, success metrics, go/no-go). **Microsoft has not endorsed
SignalForge.** Azure services appear as proposed hosting/identity options, not
as shipped certifications.

## Ask

Run a scoped enterprise POC on approved customer data sources, or review the
technical evidence package and NovaBank executive demo.
